# Aiteklabs

AI product studio in Europe. Software engineering, generative and agentic AI, security and cloud infrastructure. We ship products of our own and build products for clients on a Cloudflare-native stack.

Website: https://aiteklabs.com

## Products

| Product | What it does |
|---|---|
| [grantledger](https://grantledger.eu) | The open ledger of public funding in Europe. Grants, loans, guarantees and tax credits from 35 official feeds in 21 countries plus EU programmes, collected nightly, searchable in eight languages, screened against a company profile with rules you can read. Normalised data CC0. |
| [Replio](https://replio.eu) | AI voice role-play training for sales, support and leadership teams. |
| [AI PhotoBooth](https://aiphotobooth.eu) | AI portrait activation for events, GDPR-first, shown at Web Summit 2024 and 2025. |
| [Kampio](https://kampio.eu) | Field marketing reports with GPS-proof offline capture and AI-generated branded reports. |
| [HoldsUp](https://holdsup.app) | Fact-checking of health, business and history podcasts against published research. |
| [Clopinette](https://clopinette.app) | Cloudflare-native AI agent with persistent memory and tools. |
| [Doppelshot](https://doppelshot.com) | AI headshots, anime and movie poster portraits from one selfie, then talking, singing and moving photos. First one free. |
| [GasMap](https://gasmap.eu) | Free live fuel price map for eight European countries from official data. Loyalty cards, email price alerts, no ads. |
